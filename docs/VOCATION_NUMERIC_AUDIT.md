# Vocation numeric audit

Audit date: 2026-08-28  
Scope: Dragon Quest VII Reimagined; platform and patch unknown

## Two-source verified

- 163 rank-cost cells across 24 vocations match between the dq_st vocation
  compendium and the individually named Dragon Quest Wiki Reimagined tables.
- All 234 numeric stat-modifier cells are independently matched by dq_st and
  hyperWiki and normalized as percentages. Dragon Quest Wiki matches 162 of
  them and differs on 72.
- Each accepted row stores both sources and exact table locators.

## Third-source adjudicated

- 72 Dragon Quest Wiki stat-modifier cells disagree with the matching dq_st
  and hyperWiki values. Their claims remain visible; the conflicts resolve to
  the independently matching pair.
- Jester's seven matching rank costs sum to 400, while Dragon Quest Wiki's
  headline total says 405. hyperWiki independently publishes the seven
  increments, whose sum is 400, so the total conflict resolves to 400 while
  retaining the 405 claim.
- Conflicting numeric cells do not replace the existing qualitative Game8
  directions.

## Single-source or no numeric ladder

- **Partial ladder:** Wolf Boy ranks 7 and 8 match at 70 and 80 points in dq_st
  and Dragon Quest Wiki, with a 150 cumulative total. Those two cells are
  promoted; ranks 2–6 remain unnormalized because HyperWiki's numeric costs
  conflict with the other sources' no-cost/story progression presentation.
- **No positive-cost ladder:** Destiny's Dancer and Chevalier use zero/no-cost
  personal progress tables, so no positive rank-cost ladder applies.

## Evidence caveat

The independently matching tables are maintained guide sites, not direct
current-patch game captures. Platform and patch remain unknown. A legible
in-game status screen would provide stronger evidence than the current
two-source resolution and should supersede it if it disagrees.
