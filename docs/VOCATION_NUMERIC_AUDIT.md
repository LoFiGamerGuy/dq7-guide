# Vocation numeric audit

Audit date: 2026-08-28  
Scope: Dragon Quest VII Reimagined; platform and patch unknown

## Two-source verified

- 161 rank-cost cells across 23 vocations match between the dq_st vocation
  compendium and the individually named Dragon Quest Wiki Reimagined tables.
- 162 of 234 numeric stat-modifier cells match exactly. Only these matching
  cells are normalized as numeric percentages.
- Each accepted row stores both sources and exact table locators.

## Conflicting

- 72 stat-modifier cells disagree and remain separate, unresolved claims.
- Jester's seven matching rank costs sum to 400, while Dragon Quest Wiki's
  headline total says 405. The rank cells are accepted; the total conflict is
  not resolved.
- Conflicting numeric cells do not replace the existing qualitative Game8
  directions.

## Single-source or no numeric ladder

- **Single source:** Wolf Boy publishes only two dq_st numeric increments;
  Dragon Quest Wiki labels its rank increments unavailable. No rank-cost rows
  are promoted.
- **No positive-cost ladder:** Destiny's Dancer and Chevalier use zero/no-cost
  personal progress tables, so no positive rank-cost ladder applies.

## Evidence caveat

The two independent tables are maintained guide wikis, not direct current-patch
game captures. Platform and patch remain unknown. A legible in-game status and
rank screen should adjudicate the 72 disputed stat cells and Jester total.
